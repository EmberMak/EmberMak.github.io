The Way of the Sword

Iaido · 1st Dan (Shodan) · Kanagawa Kendo Federation · 4 Oct 2026

One draw. One cut. No wasted motion.

Iaido is the art of drawing the sword and cutting in a single movement. Every kata is practised thousands of times until nothing is left but intent and precision.

I write code the same way: understand the problem, remove what isn't needed, then make one clean, deliberate change, and test it until it holds.
