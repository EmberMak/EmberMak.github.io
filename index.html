About

Game & web developer, Singapore.

I design and build games solo, from code and art to audio and packaging: ink-wash samurai duels, dark-fantasy parry sagas, open-world pixel adventures and action RPGs. Each one is small, fast and playable in the browser or as a few-MB Android download.

On the web side, I added CSRF protection and data back-ups to a company reporting system during my IT internship at Kuai Da Trade. Through the Venture & Tech Summer Program (VTSP) I worked on AI-driven investment models, company simulations and a competitor-strategy project.

I served as Student Council President at Nan Hua High School and represented my school at the Student Leadership Convention 2023. I also work as a night and automotive photographer and hold a 1st Dan in iaido.

Chapter IV

Iaido

Iaido · 1st Dan (Shodan) · Kanagawa Kendo Federation · 4 Oct 2026

Discipline, focus and precision.

Iaido is the Japanese art of drawing the sword and cutting in a single, controlled movement. Each form is repeated until every unnecessary motion is gone.

I apply the same discipline to engineering: understand the problem fully, remove what isn't needed, make one clean and deliberate change, then test it until it holds.
